Dear participant,

Thank you for participating in 'Say Cheese' at the OK Centre for Contemporary Art, Linz, Austria during March 2003.

Whenever 'Say Cheese' took place in the past, it was a singular event with participants booking well in advance to create a sense of commitment and 'contract'. In Linz 'Say cheese' was different in that it was part of the opening event and no booking took place. I think this open-endness of queuing rather than booking gave it a certain 'buzz' or urgency not dissimilar to a club queue. This urgency sipped into the bedroom and I/Marcus felt a surreal sense of continuous excitement.

Many of the interactions felt incredibly heart warming for me - with locals from Linz and Israelis. I particularly liked the Dynamic of interacting with three 'pairs' of mothers and daughters, together or separately.

'Say Cheese' in an on-going experiment attempting to create Meaning. Meaning is explored through the brief encounter between Marcus and a participant in the bedroom.

I am not interested in creating a specific meaning; rather I am interested to speculate on the nature of meaning (or non meaning) and intimacy whilst encountering the 'Other'.

'Say Cheese' was performed during 2001-3 in London, Berlin, Ljublijana, NYC, Liverpool, Bristol and Linz, Austria.

If you'd like to give me feedback please email me on oreet@amity.co.uk
