Dear participant,

Thank you for taking part in the live interaction 'Say Cheese'. I found the interaction in Berlin very rewarding, it seems like people explored 'the moment' with great intensity. Few people used the drawings, but most wanted to do their own thing. The feedback that kept coming was that the interaction seemed too short. The time allocated was 4 minutes ( 3min previously in London) and It was probably about 6-8 with each participant. I have been thinking about the time element a lot, and whether I should make it longer for New York, but I do still feel that the interaction has to keep a sense of a 'moment', otherwise it becomes an encounter and loses the sense of heighten reality. For NY I will probably keep it at five minutes. The interaction and show will take place in Brooklyn during July 2002. If you'd like to respond in any way please email me, oreet@amity.co.uk

Best regards

Oreet/Marcus
