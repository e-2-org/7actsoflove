Dear Participant,

Thank you for taking part in the live interaction 'Say Cheese' at Foxy Productions domestic bedroom, Brooklyn, July 2002.

Say Cheese has taken place in London, Berlin, Ljubljana and NYC and will conclude at the Liverpool Biennial in September 2002.

In NYC the interactions were slightly different in that I knew most of the participants, which created a particular sense of intimacy and performativity. Also in NYC I have started to document the interactions on video for a possible future use. The video footage looks at times intense, theatrical and surreal, at others curious, occasionally camp and funny, and once very dark. There are some cinematic moments there worth exploring.

Hope you like the photograph. I welcome feedback on email; oreet@amity.co.uk

Best regards

Oreet/Marcus
