Dear Oreet,

The performance was very intriguing and provoked lots of thoughts, which is an indicator for something good.

I don't know if you've noticed, but present with Marcus Fisher and myself was also 'ima' (Invisible Mother-Artist). I marked her presence over the photograph with yellow. I wanted to ask you some questions about how you met Marcus Fisher. Is this something that you chose, or did it choose you? It seems to me that you have chosen a fiction-persona or alter-ego as far as possible from who you are, yet this is where you feel safe, comfortable, so much so that you are happy to meet with complete strangers on the bed of an establish, cutting-edge artist!

In my case 'ima' has introduced herself to me. 'ima' is very close to (me- participant name) but not identical (thank goodness). Some time I don't know who is speaking, me or her. other times I am aware that we are swapping voices, even actions.

One artist suggested that I'll try to speak with 'ima', but so far it seems to work only as long as we keep to ourselves. 'ima' sent a 'one month e-mail diary' to a one person audience called 'Julie' (which is a true friend of mine). This was a collection of writings that came about as a result of 'ima's recording her daily experiences. For example, the sound of a vacuum cleaner was recorded, then transformed into a transcript that looked like this:

Tztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztz ztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztztzt etc'.

If you like I can send you a page or two of that diary. (This work was repeated exactly on the same dates, a year later. However, this time there was more than a one person audience. Anyway, Thank you very much, I liked the videos, and regret not seeing the other films (Army, specially ), I hope to meet you again and see and speak more.
