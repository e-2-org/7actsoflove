Dear Oreet

Thank you so much for your performance at 'Home' and particularly the wonderful photos which I shall treasure as a lovely record of our meeting which felt sexy intimate nurturing relaxed and anarchically thought provoking. Why should not our identities and personas not be swappable each to the other Why should not we not all go method acting in another skin and be all races and all colours and all shapes and all beings at all places and all times and everywhere infinity onwards.

Is this not the nature of things?

Your work put for me a frame around the nature of a meeting an interaction a human exchange a jangling frisson of electric chemical nerve messages as two personalities shadow each other memories experiences each to the other glide past and are we not even a little changed? Is this not something a little miraculous like a birth?

Your work is a gift and you are a giver

Love and thanks
