Dear Participant,

Thank you for taking part in the Live interaction 'Say Cheese', Ljubljana, May 2002.

The interaction took place on a bed in an hotel room and the resulting photographs were shown at Kapelica Gallery.

'Say Cheese' in an on-going experiment attempting to create Meaning. Meaning is explored through the brief encounter between us in the bedroom.

Meaning might be created through a physical contact, a non-contact, a narrative contact or a visual one focusing on the photograph.

A participant in one interaction suggested we jump on the bed, we jumped and jumped... it was great fun. The universal pleasure of jumping together on a bed, in a child-like state produced meaning.

Another participant asked me to hug her in a way that created immediate shared intimacy.

I am not interested in creating a specific meaning, rather I am interested to speculate on the nature o f meaning whilst encountering the 'Other'.

If you'd like to give me feedback please email me on oreet@amity.co.uk
