20/3/03

Dear participant,

Thank you for participating in 'Say Cheese' at the Arnolfini during February 2003.

For the first time the interaction took place in a constructed bedroom in the gallery, rather than a domestic or hotel bedroom. It felt to me like a studio shot, which I liked a lot. Since the interaction as a whole is a construct, a constructed intimacy, so was the bedroom a construct, a physical one.

'Say Cheese' in an on-going experiment attempting to create Meaning. Meaning is explored through the brief encounter between Marcus and a participant in the bedroom.

Meaning might be created through a physical contact, a non-contact, a narrative contact or a visual one focusing on the photograph.

I am not interested in creating a specific meaning; rather I am interested to speculate on the nature of meaning and intimacy whilst encountering the 'Other'.

'Say Cheese' was performed during 2002-3 in London, Berlin, Ljublijana, NYC, Liverpool, Bristol and Linz, Austria.

If you'd like to give me feedback please email me on oreet@amity.co.uk

I hope you like the photo.
