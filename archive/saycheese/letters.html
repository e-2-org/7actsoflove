Thank you for taking part in the live interaction 'Say Cheese'. I found the interaction in Berlin very rewarding, it seems like people explored 'the moment' with great intensity. Dear participant,

Thank you for participating in 'Say Cheese' at the Arnolfini during February 2003.

For the first time the interaction took place in a constructed bedroom in the gallery, rather than a domestic or hotel bedroom. It felt to me like a studio shot, which I liked a lot. Since the interaction as a whole is a construct, a constructed intimacy, so was the bedroom a construct, a physical one.

'Say Cheese' in an on-going experiment attempting to create Meaning. Meaning is explored through the brief encounter between Marcus and a participant in the bedroom.

Meaning might be created through a physical contact, a non-contact, a narrative contact or a visual one focusing on the photograph.

I am not interested in creating a specific meaning; rather I am interested to speculate on the nature of meaning and intimacy whilst encountering the 'Other'.

'Say Cheese' was performed during 2002-3 in London, Berlin, Ljublijana, NYC, Liverpool, Bristol and Linz, Austria.

06/04/03

Dear participant,

Thank you for participating in 'Say Cheese' at the OK Centre for Contemporary Art, Linz, Austria during March 2003.

Whenever 'Say Cheese' took place in the past, it was a singular event with participants booking well in advance to create a sense of commitment and 'contract'. In Linz 'Say cheese' was different in that it was part of the opening event and no booking took place. I think this open-endness of queuing rather than booking gave it a certain 'buzz' or urgency not dissimilar to a club queue. This urgency sipped into the bedroom and I/Marcus felt a surreal sense of continuous excitement.

Many of the interactions felt incredibly heart warming for me - with locals from Linz and Israelis. I particularly liked the Dynamic of interacting with three 'pairs' of mothers and daughters, together or separately.

'Say Cheese' in an on-going experiment attempting to create Meaning. Meaning is explored through the brief encounter between Marcus and a participant in the bedroom.

I am not interested in creating a specific meaning; rather I am interested to speculate on the nature of meaning (or non meaning) and intimacy whilst encountering the 'Other'.

'Say Cheese' was performed during 2001-3 in London, Berlin, Ljublijana, NYC, Liverpool, Bristol and Linz, Austria.

16/11/2001

Dear participant,

Thank you for coming to Salon 17 at 'Home' and taking a photo with me.

The live-interaction 'Say Cheese' took place for the first time at 'Home' November 2001. Some encounters where intimate and intense, some kinky, some confessional, some a little awkward, and some heart-warming. Like life. Some people came with a concrete idea for a photograph, some wanted me to direct them.

My 'rules' were to be in control of the piece, to do anything participants wanted me to do ( apart from serious injury) and always ask participants before doing anything with them. It was important for me not to have any agenda concerning what kind of photos/encounters I wanted, I wanted to be with each participant on their own terms.

The photos made me laugh. Some of the photographs are very beautiful, some say nothing of what has been said or felt - one woman wanted me to tell her that I didn't love her anymore and the fictional verbal exchange became so engaging that it seems we forgot to press the shutter altogether.

I've been thinking about the interaction since it happened, in terms of its development: Should the emphasis be on the photo or the actual encounter? What if the poses will become more structured, preconceived, referencing some 'classic bedroom shoots', (some of Nan Goldin's images come to mind)? should I introduce an option of 'acting' fictional dialogues? How about colour photos?

'Say cheese' will take place for the second time in Berlin, January 2002, in the curator's bedroom, as part of a one person show. (Galerie Stil+Bruch 21stJan -3rdFeb). Some of the photographs from the 'Home' event will be hand- printed for the show/cd-rom in Berlin.

If you don't wish your photo to be used publicly let me know soon via email/phone/post. Also if you want to respond to the interaction please email me.

Best regards

Oreet/Marcus
